Frequently asked questions

The alarm didn't ring. Check that Alarms are allowed for Waqr in iOS Settings, and keep your iPhone charged overnight.

My code won't scan. Print at 100% scale, avoid glossy paper, and use the torch in the scanner. Test-scan the code where you stuck it, with the light you'll have in the morning.

I can't reach my code. Turn on the Emergency exit in Settings: solving a few sums stops the alarm.

Can I use a barcode instead of printing? Yes. Register any barcode you own (toothpaste, cereal box, book) from the Codes tab.
